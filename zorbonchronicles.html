THE CHRONICLES OF ZORBON

a real-time command of Commander Gloongook

Three threats bear down on the Zorbonian people:

Mitt's Forehead — a rising, radiant menace. Fund Incinerators from the sidebar to burn its power back. If it ever reaches 100%, Zorbon falls immediately.
The M.I.T.T. — hostile fighters swarming your sector. Fly Gloongook with WASD and fire missiles with the arrow keys to shoot them down. Each missile costs 25 Zip Zaps. If M.I.T.T. Incursion ever reaches 100%, Zorbon falls immediately.
The Sagittarians — lobbying the Allied Council for entry. Fund Diplomacy from the sidebar to quiet them. This meter doesn't end the game early — instead it decides how hard the final vote will be.

Random events interrupt with something that has already happened, then ask how you respond. Each choice previews its possible outcomes and odds — but the result is rolled live.

Survive the clock without Forehead or M.I.T.T. hitting 100%, and it goes to the Allied Council vote. Zorba-274 (you) automatically votes against admitting the Sagittarians. You then get 3 rounds to personally lobby the Ambassadors of Andromeda, Zorba-275, and Milky Way — while Sagittarian lobbyists work against you. Win if at least one ambassador joins your "no" vote.
